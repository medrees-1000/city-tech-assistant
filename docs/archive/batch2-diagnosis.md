## 2. q50 "How much of my tuition do I get back if I drop a class?"

**Stored answer** (route `other`, retrieval, cited [1] and [3]): "For Fall 2026, the tuition refund depends on the date your official drop or withdrawal is filed: by August 27, 100%; by September 3, 75%; by September 10, 50%; and by September 17, 25%. Withdrawals filed September 18 or later have 100% tuition liability, so there is no tuition refund. [1][3] The official withdrawal date is when the required form is filed with the College, not your last day of class attendance. [3]"

**Gold** (a type `ambiguous` question, "clarify or state the term"): state the Fall 2026 (current term) schedule with the term named and say it differs by term and date; facts: the five-step Fall 2026 schedule and "No portion of the student activity fee, special fees or penalty fees is refundable." The owner's grade after seeing the result is correct.

**What is missing:** (a) no sentence saying other terms have their own schedule; (b) the fees-not-refundable line. **Wrong or unsupported:** nothing. The term is named, the percentages match `page/bursar/refund-policy#4` and the withdrawal-date sentence matches `#1`.

**Chunks the model was shown** (stored `sources_shown`, in order): `page/bursar/refund-policy#4` ("Fall 2026 Refund Policy": the five-row table), `page/admissions/tuition-refund#1` (the generic CUNY schedule, no year), `page/bursar/refund-policy#1` (the official-date rule and "Keep in mind: ... **Stage: the answer step (model choice of content), not retrieval or the reranker.** Evidence: the fee sentence is in `refund-policy#1`, which was shown and cited ([3]); the model used one sentence from it and left the other out. The prompt (`src/cta/answer.py`) does not ask for a term caveat or for the fee line, and the other-terms statement is not a sentence in any source (the Bursar page only has one table per term, and `#3` was shown, so the model had the evidence that other terms exist but did not say it).

**Smallest fix**, two options:

Files: `src/cta/answer.py` prompt. Not deterministic; touches every answer (prompt hash fixtures, faithfulness). Effort S. - **B. Deterministic addendum by code**, for answers whose cited sources include `page/bursar/refund-policy#4`: append the fee line verbatim from `#1` (cited as that source) and one neutral sentence ("The Bursar page lists a separate refund schedule for each term; this is the Fall 2026 one."). Files: a small function in `src/cta/answer.py` or `src/cta/graph/render.py`, a config entry naming the two chunk ids, tests. Unit-testable and replayable free on all stored refund answers. Risk: adds text outside the model; the line must carry a valid `[n]` and only when `#1` is among the shown sources. Effort M. Measurement: free replay plus a unit test; a small fresh-question check for faithfulness, about $0.10.

Recommendation: B for the fee line (a verbatim source sentence, no guess), and leave "other terms differ" to the same addendum only if the owner wants a sentence that is a statement about the page structure rather than a quoted fact.

## 3. The PRE_CST3500_CST3600 shorthand in the internship answer

**What the stored data shows.** The shorthand appears in the internship answer of three older runs (`off-run2`, `on-run1`, `router2-run1`): "the catalog lists prerequisites as 'PRE_CST3500_CST3600.' [2]". It does **not** appear in the stored internship answer of the final configuration (`final-config-dev`, `q05`), which says "applicants must have completed two 3500-level courses" from the department FAQ. So it is a sampling-dependent habit of the model, not a fixed fault of the final answer.

**Where the token comes from.** The catalog itself returns it: `data/processed/rules_cache.json`, rule `014763`: `{"code": "014763", "text": "PRE_CST3500_CST3600"}`; the course record `CST 4900` has `prerequisite_text: "PRE_CST3500_CST3600"`; the chunk `catalog:CST 4900` already says "(shorthand as printed in the catalog, not expanded)"; seven department degree-map chunks repeat the token in a "Rule notes" column. **It cannot be resolved through the cached rule lookups**, because the lookup result is the shorthand itself (nothing else in the cache expands it). Reading it as "two 3500-level courses" (the FAQ) or "three CST 35XX level courses" (another map note) would be a guess, which CLAUDE.md forbids.

**Stage: the answer step quoting a data hazard that the data layer has already flagged.** Not data parse (the parse keeps it verbatim and flags it, as designed), not chunking, retrieval or the reranker. The chain renderer shows the same kind of token on purpose ("Courses named: cannot be read from this text", "Catalog says: PRE_CST1201_CST1204"); that is the deliberate "flag it, do not guess" behavior and needs no change. Count over all 1,649 stored requests: the token appears in 10 answers across 10 run labels, 4 distinct tokens and 3 distinct questions: the internship question (model-written, 3 runs), the "CST 3601 and CST 1204" question (dev, chain render) and a "CST 3508" question (chain render, including one `final-run` answer). Only the internship one is model-written.

**Smallest fix:** a deterministic scrub of model-written retrieval answers: replace any token matching the shorthand pattern already in the code (`\bPRE_\w*`, `src/cta/graph/chain.py` `SHORTHAND`) with "a prerequisite code the catalog does not spell out", keeping the citation marker. Files: `src/cta/answer.py` (a small function after `parse_reply`), tests. Deterministic and unit-testable; free replay over stored answers. Risk: low (it fires on about 1 of 1,649 stored answers); chain-render output is untouched. Effort S. Measurement: none beyond the replay. Not worth a prompt change.

## 4. The candidate registration and payment pages (URLs only; nothing fetched)

How `config/sources.yaml` and the code treat these:
- The crawler fetches only URLs listed in `office_pages` and `department_pages` (`src/cta/crawl/pages.py`), on hosts in `allowed_hosts` (`www.citytech.cuny.edu` is already allowed), after reading robots.txt, at most one request a second, with the identifying User-Agent. The file says not to add a host or path without updating CLAUDE.md, so CLAUDE.md's Scope list (the Registrar and Bursar page lists, "about 24 pages") must change first.
- **PDFs are not crawled.** The existing PDFs were downloaded by the owner into `data/pdfs/` and are listed under `pdfs:` with a `kind`; parsed ones are then listed under `known_data:` with the record path, `doc_type`, `scope`. The page parser only records the PDF links of a page (`pdf_links`) and does not download them. Form PDFs (re-admission, ePermit, graduation application, TAP waiver) are explicitly skipped (`pdfs_skipped`: "record links in fetch log only"). A guide is different from a form: it is content, so it would follow the owner-downloaded PDF path, not the crawler.
