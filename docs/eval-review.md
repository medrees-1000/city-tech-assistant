~~~~markdown
If you drop a class and receive a **WD grade** between **September 4 and September 17, 2026,** the course will continue to count toward your enrollment status for financial aid purposes.

**Example:**

If you begin the semester with 12 credits and drop a 3-credit course on September 17, you will remain classified as a full-time student for Federal Pell Grant, FSEOG, Federal Direct Loan, and NYS TAP eligibility.

- Your NYS TAP award may be prorated (reduced) for the semester.
- You may use the equivalent of 6 TAP points for that term.
~~~~

~~~~markdown
If you drop a class on or before **September 3, 2026,** the course will not be included when determining your enrollment status for financial aid eligibility.

Enrollment status categories include:

- Full-time (12+ Credits)
- Three-quarter-time (9-11 Credits)
- Half-time (6-8 Credits)
- Less than half-time (1-5 Credits)

**Example:**

If you are enrolled in four courses totaling **12 credits (full-time)** and drop one 3-credit course on or before September 3rd, your enrollment would be reduced to **9 credits,** making you a **three-quarter-time** enrolled student for financial aid purposes. This may affect your eligibility for programs such as the Federal Pell Grant and NYS TAP.
~~~~
